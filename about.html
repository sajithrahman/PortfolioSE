Professional background

Engineering enterprise software
with clarity and precision

Sajith Rahman is a Software Engineer based in India with over four years of specialized experience in ERP architecture, cloud business systems, and REST API engineering.

The journey from computer science to enterprise platforms

My career in engineering began with an MS in Computer Science from Government Arts College, Coimbatore. Early on, I recognized that software creates the highest value when it directly solves core operational friction for businesses.

Over the past four years, I have worked deeply within the Odoo ecosystem, spanning Community and Enterprise releases from version 12 to 19. My work includes building complex human resource management systems, automated statutory payroll calculation engines, manufacturing workflows, and multicompany accounting ledgers.

Alongside Odoo, I configure Oracle NetSuite cloud systems, authoring custom saved searches, revenue recognition pipelines, and SuiteScript client validations. I also engineer high volume REST microservices that integrate biometric hardware, digital verification platforms, and payment processors.

My approach prioritizes maintainability. I avoid fragile modifications to core framework code, ensuring every custom module is isolated, thoroughly tested, and ready for future upgrades.
